FAQ

Questions we get before someone signs on.

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring your content so AI models like ChatGPT and Gemini can find, understand, and cite it when someone asks a related question. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is closely related — it's optimizing specifically to win the direct-answer placements search engines show, like featured snippets and AI Overviews.

Technical fixes and early AEO wins can show up within a few weeks. Meaningful ranking and AI-citation growth typically builds over 3–6 months, since both search engines and language models need time to re-crawl and re-trust updated content.
